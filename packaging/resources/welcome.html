Welcome to Hush-chan 🤫

A one-knob spectral gate, run by a sweet anime girl who gets less sweet the more you turn it.

Turn the ANGER knob up and every frequency below her quiet line gets removed: hiss, hum, room tone and reverb tails. Push it hard and she removes nearly everything.

Installs VST3 and AU for macOS (Apple Silicon + Intel).

This is a library.
