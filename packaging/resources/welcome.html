Welcome to Shizuka 🤫

A one-knob spectral gate, run by a librarian who has had enough.

Turn the SHHH-O-METER up and every frequency below her quiet line gets removed: hiss, hum, room tone and reverb tails. Push it hard and she removes nearly everything.

Installs VST3 and AU for macOS (Apple Silicon + Intel).

This is a library.
